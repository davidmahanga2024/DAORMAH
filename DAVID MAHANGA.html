About Mamuya Safaris

At Mamuya Safaris, we are passionate about creating unforgettable wildlife and cultural experiences across Tanzania. Our mission is to connect travelers with the breathtaking beauty of Africa through expertly guided safaris, personalized itineraries, and exceptional customer service. From the vast plains of the Serengeti and the wildlife-rich Ngorongoro Crater to the pristine beaches of Zanzibar, we design journeys that showcase the very best of Tanzania. Whether you are seeking a luxury safari, a family adventure, a honeymoon getaway, or a customized private tour, our experienced team is dedicated to making every moment extraordinary. With a strong commitment to responsible tourism, we work closely with local communities and conservation initiatives to ensure that our safaris contribute positively to both people and nature.

Why Choose Mamuya Safaris?

Mamuya Safaris combines expert local knowledge , personalized service, and authentic Tanzanian experiences. As a family-owned company based in Arusha, we create tailor-made safaris that bring you closer to Tanzania's spectacular wildlife, vibrant cultures, and breathtaking landscapes.

Our passion, experience, and commitment to exceptional service ensure every journey becomes a lifetime memory . Welcome to Mamuya Safaris
