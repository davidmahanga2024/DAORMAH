OUR SERVICES
Mamuya Safaris specializes in private, customizable wildlife and cultural tours across Tanzania, with options for both standard and luxury experiences, plus local cultural immersion. Custom Itineraries: You can choose from pre‑set packages or design a completely tailored schedule, including specific parks, reserves, and activities . Destinations: Covers major Tanzanian national parks and reserves such as Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire, Lake Manyara, Arusha, Mkomazi, Mahale, Gombe, Ruaha, Saadani, and more . Wildlife Focus: Tailored to showcase Tanzania’s “Big Five” and other wildlife experiences, with close‑up encounters guided by local experts
